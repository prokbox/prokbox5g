# Open questions (WM-D)

*Each item: what is unknown, why it matters, and the probe that would answer it. Ordered roughly by how much they matter to a from-scratch driver or to the battery. Numbers are fixed once published; add new items at the end. Probes that write to the codec are I2C writes with the full `audiohw` init as the fallback; none can hang the unit. Anything needing a meter is held: we judge power by battery-slope A/B for now (`../hardware.md`).*

| # | Question | Why it matters | Probe |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **How the PP5022's I2C controller lays out its data bytes**: byte-wide registers four bytes apart (Rockbox) or one packed word at `0x7000_C00C` (Apple's boot driver). Both work on silicon | A from-scratch I2C driver needs the real layout; it also decides whether our decode of Apple's codec writes is right (`bring-up.md` §1.2, quirk 26) | Bench row **`i2creg`**: write codec registers both ways and hear the result (for example a volume step), then the same against the PMU, whose registers read back |
| 2 | **Which rails feed DCVDD, DBVDD, AVDD1 and AVDD2**, and whether DCVDD sits on the 1.8 V `DCUDC1` | DCVDD at 1.8 V instead of 2.5 V is the largest codec-side saving (24.0 → 17.8 mW typical) | Multimeter on pins 13, 14, 31, 26 against ground while playing (owner, read-only) (`power.md` §3) |
| 3 | **The level and wiring of CSB/GPIO1 (pin 15)** | In 2-wire mode a high level inverts `HP_COM`/`LINE_COM`, so we do not know whether our headphone common-ground feedback is on (quirk 9); the pin could also report PLL lock or temperature | Multimeter on pin 15 while running; or an A/B listen for ground noise and crosstalk with R49 `0x187` against `0x087` |
| 4 | **The soft-mute ramp time** | Pause fades and the standby entry assume the ramp is short | Mute during a steady tone and time the decay on a scope, or with the BCM's sampler on a data-carrying pin if one is found |
| 5 | **What 64× oversampling (`DACOSR128` = 0) sounds like and saves** | The datasheet calls 64× the lowest-power DAC setting but contradicts itself on noise (quirk 23) | Blind A/B listen; then a 30-minute battery-slope A/B, playing, screen off |
| 6 | **The PLL's real lock time** | Our 2 ms settle may be too long (wake latency) or too short (a click on a slow lock) | Shorten `DAC_PLL_SETTLE_US` stepwise and listen at each resume; or GPIO1 set to "PLL lock" if pin 15 is reachable (question 3) |
| 7 | **BCLK at 32 fs** (R6 `0x14D` at 44.1/48 kHz, as Apple's boot driver) | Fewer interface edges; the master-mode power row shows DBVDD at 3.5 mA with BCLK at 64 fs, so the interface clock may cost a few milliwatts (`power.md` §1). The BCM's GPIO 2 sampler and any BCM audio path see the same clock | With the output muted, write the new R6, unmute, play: listen for correct pitch and channels; count LRCK on the BCM sampler (must stay 2 × fs); then a battery-slope A/B |
| 8 | **What Apple writes to `0x7000_0018` for the codec's MCLK** (12 MHz implied by its PLL values) | A 12 MHz MCLK halves the MCLK pin's edges and lets the PLL run without the prescaler | RE: Apple's PP-side audio bring-up; then the same listen-and-count probe as 7 with R36 = `0x007` |
| 9 | **Does our power-up pop?** Ours clears `POBCTRL` early, charges VMID through 10 kΩ and never waits 100 ms (quirks 3–6) | A pop at boot is a native-feel defect; Apple follows the datasheet | Headphones on, cold boot ten times, listen; repeat with the datasheet's order (Apple's mode 4) in a test build |
| 10 | **Does a sample-rate change click?** The driver rewrites the PLL with the output live (quirk 15) | Albums that mix 44.1 and 48 kHz files | Play a 44.1 kHz track into a 48 kHz one with a quiet boundary; listen; the fix would be a soft mute around `audiohw_set_frequency()` |
| 11 | **Are there DC-blocking capacitors on OUT1?** | The driver assumes them; a capless board would need OUT2 as the mid-rail reference | Board photo or trace near pins 29/30 |
| 12 | **Does OUT2 reach the USB-C mod's audio?** Apple enables OUT2; the mod's makers say audio is wired out over USB-C | Audio out over USB-C to a car or dock without USB audio | Enable OUT2 (`audiohw_enable_lineout(true)`, R54/R55 unmuted) and listen on the USB-C audio path (as the Bluetooth bench row BT-4 does for a BT module) |
| 13 | **EQ in low-power mode: EQ4 on its own, and whether setting a peak band silently raises the DSP to full rate**; also where the digital volume sits relative to the EQ | Quirk 11; the precut is only needed if the EQ can clip before the volume attenuates | Repeat spike S1 for EQ4; battery-slope A/B with a peak preset against flat; a 0 dBFS tone with a +12 dB band at low volume, listen for clipping with precut 0 |
| 14 | **Why 2 of 6 resumes were late with the MCLK and I2S gated** (AW-5) | Gating MCLK is the deeper standby the datasheet's `SLEEP` bit is a substitute for | Re-run `awi2` with a log line at each step of the wake (ungate, PLL on, first DMA request), and variant a (MCLK only) |
| 15 | **The limiter's timings at rates other than 44.1 kHz** | The limiter and its boost are candidates for the Volume Limit and Sound Check | Tone bursts at 44.1 and 48 kHz with a fixed attack/decay; scope the envelope |
| 16 | **Can the PP's I2S block carry 24-bit samples?** (`IISCONFIG[9:8]`, FIFO format) | The codec side is ready; hi-res sources would keep their depth | PP-side probe W-8 (`../hardware-features.md`); then a 24-bit tone with R4 `WL` = 24 |
| 17 | **Auto-mute: does it hide idle hiss, and does its release pop?** | A free silence between tracks | R10 with `AMUTE`; listen through gaps and track starts. 0.9.0 (#327): wired behind `DAC_AMUTE_DEFAULT 0` and `dac_set_amute()`, kept across rate changes, standby and mutes (`tests/dacosr/test_eqlev.c`); bench row `amute` decides the default |
| 18 | **What the undocumented delay bits do** (`DELEN`, `OUT1DEL`, `OUT2DEL`, `DELEN2`, `OUT3ENDEL`, `OUT4ENDEL`) | Apple uses three of them in its anti-pop power-up (quirk 20) | Sibling datasheets (WM8983, WM8985) for the same names; then an A/B listen at power-up |
