# Where ProkBox deliberately differs from the iPod Classic 7G (software 2.0.5)

*Living ledger. First written 2026-10-09 from `main` `66bd3b4c51` plus branch `p11` `ec1e371a51` (Phase 11, merging). The north star is "as close to stock as we can manage, in every way" (CLAUDE.md), so every entry here is an exception that needs a reason and a decider. **Update at every phase close** (`docs/process.md`, "Ledgers"), and whenever an owner decision moves a line in either direction.*

The sibling ledger for the Rockbox side is `docs/changes-from-rockbox.md`.

**How to read an entry.**
- **Decision**: what we do instead of stock.
- **Reason**: why.
- **Who / when**: the decider and the date, as recorded in the docs, commit messages or tracker. "Owner" is the project owner. Where a doc does not record a date, the entry says so.
- **Status**: *permanent* (a principled difference: trademark, legal, original art, our hardware) or *provisional* (the owner may revisit; the default is easy to change in source).
- **P11** means the entry lives on branch `p11` and merges with Phase 11.
- Anything not documented is under "To confirm" (section 5), not guessed.
