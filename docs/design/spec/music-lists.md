# Music menu & browse lists

*Split from `7g-spec.md` "Global chrome" and "Menu trees" (2026-10-07). List pitch numbers are in [index.md](index.md) "List geometry".*

## List rendering

- **Lists:** white background, **25 px row pitch**, first row starts right under the header (y 20). Black left-aligned text at **x = 7**, in **Helvetica Bold** on the real 7G (cap height 11–12 px ≈ 16 px). We use TeX Gyre Heros Bold at 16 px.
  - **Chevron only on the selected row, and only if that row leads somewhere** (a submenu or another screen): a white `›` about 6×11 px, 6 px from the right edge of the list. Unselected rows show no chevron. Selected leaf rows show none either: songs, Resume and chapters, hold-SELECT items, notes, inline-value settings such as Repeat (photos `songs-list-scrollbar-b.jpg`, `audiobook-open-chapters-b.jpg`, `hold-select-popup-np-b.jpg`, `settings-03-repeat.jpg`). *(This contradicts the old spec's "every row has a chevron". That was 5G behaviour.)*
  - The selected row is a blue vertical gradient, 23 px visible (y 20–42 for row 0): a 1 px light top line ≈ #91C7E9, body ≈ #3A9BDC → #226DCA, and a 1–2 px darker bottom edge ≈ #254F7F (main.png; main-alt.png is lighter, #3EABE3 → #1685D3). White text. Per the owner we draw it lighter and glossier: sky blue #4CB8F0 → #1C8CDC with the top ~45% blended toward white (see "Visual language").
  - Settings-style rows show their value right-aligned in **blue** (e.g. "Off", "All", "First", "Beep"; a mid-blue ≈ the selection bar's lower colour), right edge ≈12 px inside the list column *(≈)*. On the selected row the value turns **white** and there is no chevron. *(Was "gray"; photos `settings-02-shuffle.jpg`, `extras-calendars.jpg`, `extras-stopwatch.jpg` show blue.)*
  - Album/podcast lists use two-line rows with a thumbnail (≈44 px) on the left, a bold title and a gray regular subtitle ("13 Songs" or the artist), about 4 per screen; the selection bar starts **right of the thumbnail** (photo `albums-list-twoline-b.jpg`). **Song lists are two-line too** (title bold / artist gray), **without** thumbnails, about 5 per screen *(≈44 px pitch)*, with thin gray separator lines (photo `songs-list-scrollbar-b.jpg`). Artists and the Music menu are single-line.
  - Truncation appends "..." to the cut text (even after a period: "arr...."), no fade.
  - Long selected titles marquee-scroll after a short delay.

- **Scrolling:**
  - The list moves smoothly at the pixel level with the wheel and accelerates with wheel speed.
  - A scrollbar shows at the right edge of the list column on lists longer than the screen: a **7 px gray rounded thumb** (owner, device #58: about one lowercase letter of the list font; the photos suggested ≈3–4 px), no visible track, flush with the screen's right edge, or 1 px from the list half's right edge in a split, drawn over the rows *(≈; photos `songs-list-scrollbar-b.jpg`, `settings-04-main-menu.jpg`)*. Thumb length ∝ visible/total.
  - In long sorted lists, fast scrolling shows a big **letter overlay** in the middle of the screen (the 7G quick-scroll letter): a **black rounded square ≈70–80 px** with a big white bold letter, the list still visible around it. In 2.0.5 numbers and symbols sort **after Z**, and their overlay shows **"123"** with the digits staggered diagonally (1 high, 2 middle, 3 low), **not "#"** (photos `quickscroll-letter-S-b.jpg`, `quickscroll-123-b.jpg`).
  - **Fast spin: whole-row jumps (#58, owner device feedback, 2026-10-09).** On the 5.5G's slow LCD a pixel glide at high wheel speed smears and ghosts the glyphs. Above `UI_LIST_JUMP_V` (24 clicks/s, about one turn a second) the list moves in whole-row jumps with no glide frames in between, at most one frame per `UI_LIST_JUMP_TICKS` (HZ/20); the positions in between are dropped, never queued. The letter overlay shows as before. Below `UI_LIST_JUMP_V_EXIT` (16) it glides again; stand-in thumbnails are read once the wheel has rested `UI_LIST_JUMP_SETTLE` (HZ/4). The constants are in `apps/ipodui/list.h`, to tune on the device (P10). Normal speeds glide unchanged.
  - The **selection bar animates** between rows over ≈1–2 frames (**≤80 ms**), with the text colour cross-fading; it never jumps (video frame `mov-main-selection-anim.jpg`).

## Menu trees — Music

- **Music:** Cover Flow, Playlists (with On-The-Go), Artists, Albums, Compilations, Songs, Genres, Composers, **Audiobooks**, Search.
  - Stock 2.0.5 (photo `music-menu-b.jpg`, video `photo 1190.mov`): **Cover Flow, Genius Mixes, Playlists, Artists, Albums, Compilations, Songs, Genres, Composers, Audiobooks, Search**. **Genius Mixes sits directly under Cover Flow**; we hide it while Genius is skipped (catalogue §5.6, owner decision pending), keeping that slot if it is ever adapted. Compilations appears only when enabled, between Albums and Songs. The Music menu is a split screen, with covers on the right.

- **Music hold menu (hold Select on the main menu's Music row; hidden maintenance, not in the 7G).** A full-screen hold menu (same pattern as the song hold menu) with three rows: **Rebuild Cache**, **Build Artwork**, **Cancel**. (`LANG_IPODUI_REBUILD_CACHE`, title case like every 7G menu row, replacing upstream's "Rebuild cache" on 2026-10-18; `LANG_IPODUI_BUILD_ARTWORK`; `LANG_CANCEL_0`.) Either action first stops playing or paused audio, then opens a persistent progress page that closes when the work is done. While the work waits for a charge ("Charge to Continue") MENU leaves the page and the work carries on by itself once the battery allows (usbfix budget review M2).
  - **Rebuild Cache:** a full update of the library, not a destructive rebuild (`Q_UPDATE`). The old database stays in use until the new one commits; the CPU is boosted; art work then waits for the fresh RAM library. Page: **Updating Library...** with a percentage when the scan can estimate one, then the artwork phases below.
  - **Build Artwork:** walks the current library and refreshes only missing or stale covers. It does not scan or rewrite the database.
  - **Artwork phases**, each with its own label; counted phases show done/total of items checked (attempted failures included), phases with no known total show only the label, never a made-up percentage: **Preparing Artwork...**, **Requested Covers...**, **Updating Artwork...** (the album table, done/total albums), **Updating Panels...**, **Updating Chapter Art...**, **Updating Book Cache...**, **Finishing Artwork...**. A slow single decode can hold a counter still; a phase change explains a counter reset.
  - **Wait reasons** replace an ambiguous `0 / total`: **Waiting for Library...** (library commit or reload), **Artwork Paused** (an art viewer is open), **Waiting for Playback...**, **Charge to Continue** (low battery), **Artwork Waiting...** (a general safe-resume wait).
  - The page redraws once a second so display DMA does not compete with the scan. The work yields at once to USB, power-off, low battery and active playback; an interrupted pass is requested again after USB disconnect. A scan in progress is also waited for at the art builder's work and I/O boundaries (an in-flight decode may finish).
  - Differs from the 7G on purpose: see [`../changes-from-7g.md`](../changes-from-7g.md) 2.35.

- **Artists → Albums → Songs**, with an "All Albums" / "All Songs" row at the top of each list.

- **Search:** full-width list; top row (magnifier tile) **Search** / "No Search Results" → "Results for: h"; result rows with a type icon; a dark rounded letter strip near the bottom (≈y 190–215) with a white text field at the left and the alphabet, the current letter in a blue rounded box (photos `search-empty-b.jpg`, `search-results-b.jpg`).

- **Scrollbar (owner, 2026-10-09, after using it on the device):** there is **no track**. It is just a grey, semi-transparent (see-through) bar that moves along the right edge of the list, from just below the header bar to the bottom of the screen. Its length is proportional to the visible fraction. Remove any track or groove we draw behind the thumb.
- **Scrollbar size and inset (owner, 2026-10-09, device):** a few px wider than now, about the width of one lowercase letter of the list font (~7–8 px), and closer to the edge it sits against (the left panel's right edge in split screens, or the screen's right edge), so there's less of a gap.
- **Now-playing marker (owner, 2026-10-12, from the stock 7G):** in song lists (Albums › album, Songs, Artists › album › songs, playlists, On-The-Go) the row of the track that is playing now shows the **speaker-with-waves glyph** at the right edge, the same glyph and size as the loud end of Now Playing's volume bar (`NPG_SPEAKER_WAVES`). **Black on an unselected row, white on the selected (blue gel) row.** It replaces nothing else on the row. Extend it (our addition) to Audiobooks: the playing book's row, and inside a book the **current chapter's** row. It moves when the track or chapter changes, redrawing only the two rows involved.
