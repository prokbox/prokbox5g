# System screens

*Split from `7g-spec.md` "Global chrome" and "Behaviors" (2026-10-07).*

## Dark full-screen screens

- **Dark full-screen screens** (USB connected, About, Brightness, Screen Lock, and charging): a **dark header** (≈22 px, #333739 → #191D1F) with the title **centred** in white (or the clock with Time in Title, or the device name on About) and a light battery outline; body a slate-to-navy vertical gradient (lighter top, near-black bottom); content centred in white. Photos `usb-connected-b.jpg`, `about-1.jpg`, `brightness.jpg`, `extras-screenlock-new-combination.jpg`.

## Behaviors
- Boot goes to the logo, then the main menu, restoring the last screen and playback state.
- **Boot screen (owner photo `XCShMisR6KMNUYmB.webp`, 2026-10-07):** a blue-grey vertical gradient background (confirmed in person, not a camera artefact), with a silvery, shaded, metallic 3D logo about 63–70 px tall, centred horizontally and slightly above centre. Our glyph is the "wheel" in that metallic treatment; see `docs/design/boot-logo.md`.
- Resume:
  - Music resumes its queue.
  - **Every audiobook remembers its own position.**
  - Podcasts remember their position too.
- Holding PLAY puts the iPod to sleep. The backlight wakes on any button.
- **Unlit clock screen ("screensaver", owner, 2026-10-07):** when the backlight times out, the screen switches to a high-contrast, essentially **black-and-white** face, readable by ambient light without the backlight:
  - the **time in large block digits**;
  - below it, a **play or pause glyph** (whichever applies; none when stopped ⚠️);
  - a **battery icon**.

  It is slightly stylised but reads as black on white for contrast. It updates **once a minute** (no seconds, no animation, never boosts) and redraws only the changed digits. **LCD sleep (BCM shutdown) follows about 60 s after the backlight goes off** (owner: "a minute or two"; shortened from 90 s for battery, owner 2026-10-12), and the panel is then blank until a button wakes it. **Dismissal (owner, 2026-10-07):**
  - **Sliding Hold on brings the face up at once**, from any screen, Now Playing included (owner, 2026-10-11, #65): the marquee and every animation stop. Over the USB screen and in developer mode it doesn't.
  - Once shown, the face **stays up even if the backlight comes back on without a button press**: for example sliding Hold **on** (the lock), plugging in a charger, or other backlight-on events. It is then simply lit.
  - It is dismissed by **any button press**, or by sliding Hold **off** (coming off the lock), which restores the previous screen.
  - ⚠️ The exact layout, and whether a button press that dismisses it also acts, wait for an owner photo or video (default: the dismissing press is consumed).
- **Video failure prompt (ours; owner, 2026-10-13):** errors are normally logged, never shown (CLAUDE.md rule 1). **Exception: a video that fails to play** (refused at open, the player failing mid-play, or, #340, a start that never gets going: the sound not playing within 4 s (10 s on a hard drive), the sound never reaching the video's file within 6 s (12 s on a hard drive), or the sound running out with no picture ever shown after a decode error) stops everything and shows the light prompt (`setwidgets.h`: the Reset Settings / New Software look) titled "Can't Play Video", a heading and one or two lines for the reason group, and one OK; SELECT or MENU returns to the list. No codes on screen; the code goes to `ipodui.log`. See `docs/design/changes-from-7g.md` 2.34.
- **The library page (#237 and #157 in 0.8.1; `apps/ipodui/updscreen.c`; owner, 2026-11-09: whenever the menu shows after a cold boot or an unplug, all the music is there and current).** One full-screen page in the 7G's "OK to Disconnect" look (the owner's 7G capture `usb-ok-to-disconnect`): the dark USB body and header, the orange disc, a bold title on the USB screen's baseline (187), a **rounded bar** (a light glossy fill on a dark recessed track, 200 px wide from x 60, 11 rows from y 197) and under it one **step in plain words** (Reg 12, light grey, baseline 224); never counts or technical terms.
  - **First boot with no library** (a first install, or a build a power loss or USB cut): the page comes up over the main menu at once, titled **"Preparing Your Library"**, the disc showing the sync arrows standing still. Steps: "Finding your music..." (the count of the folders), "Adding your songs..." (the walk, nearly all of the wait), "Sorting your library...", "Almost ready..." (the load and the lists). It stays until the library is loaded, then the main menu (a plain slide). If nothing runs and no library comes for 10 s it logs that and shows the menu.
  - **After an unplug** from a session in which the computer changed files (a write into the disk's data area, #314): titled **"OK to Disconnect"**, the disc with the ejected pictogram, up on the first frame after the release (#315, 0.8.3). A session that changed no file runs no check and shows no page. One that changed none while a check or build was left unfinished at the connect: for its first second the USB frame stays, and work over by then goes straight to the menu with no slide. Steps (#276, 0.8.2): "Checking for changes..." (the read-only look at the folders), "Updating your songs..." (the walk), then "Sorting your library..." and "Almost ready..." as above. The album art is not part of the wait, and none of it runs while the library is being made (owner, 2026-11-10: library building takes utter priority; the art fills in behind the menu).
  - **The bar** follows real work units, never time: a first build's count of the tree gives the walk its scale; after an unplug the last whole walk does, and with none yet this boot (#315) the walk fills a bar that slows towards its end (about 80 % at the end of a typical tree), so it moves from the first folder. Each stage owns a share of the bar (first build: count 2 %, walk to 96 %, commit 98.5 %, load and lists 99.5 %; unplug: check 25 %, walk 65 %, commit 90 %, lists 99 %); a stage that comes round again starts from where the bar is, so it never goes back, and it is full only when the work is over. **A sign of life (#276):** while work runs a soft light (48 px wide, brighter over the fill than over the track) travels along the bar once every 2 s, so no step looks frozen however long it lasts; none while the build waits for power, none once full. It redraws only its band (between steps only the bar's rows), at most 8 times a second.
  - **Keys:** every key, the wheel and hold MENU do nothing; hold PLAY still sleeps or powers off (a cut build is made again at the next boot). USB plugged in replaces it; after that session the first-boot page comes back if there is still no library.
  - **Power (owner, 2026-11-09):** on battery below 15 % a first build starts only on a charger, and one under way stops at its next folder when the charger goes; the step then reads "Connect to power to finish your library." and the bar holds until power is back.
- Any other library update (Rebuild, the boot's check) shows only the header's busy glyph (3.20), never a screen.
- **USB connected (owner's 7G photos `usb-connected` / photo `usb-connected-b`, 2026-10-06):** a full-screen **dark blue gradient** (lighter top, near-black bottom) with a dark header showing the **clock centred** (Time in Title) and the battery, with a charging plug, at right. In the centre is a **glossy orange disc**, lighter at the top and darker toward the bottom, which never changes. Below it: **"Connected"** (bold) and **"Eject before disconnecting."**
  - **Stock 2.0.5 behaviour (owner, 2026-10-06):** the glyph is a black **30-pin dock-connector pictogram** while there is **no data transfer** (photo `usb-connected-b.jpg`, photo `usb-connected-b`), and **two black sync arrows spinning** while there **is** data I/O (photo `usb-connected.jpg`, photo `usb-connected`). We reproduce exactly that, keyed off USB mass-storage activity. Both glyphs are our own drawings.
  - *Implementation:* draw the disc once, and redraw only the glyph area per frame at a low frame rate; never boost; stop while the backlight is off.
- **USB screen status (owner, 2026-10-08):** today our build still shows the **stock Rockbox USB screen**. It must be replaced by the native 7G-style screen above, in Phase 8 or earlier.
  - **Connector glyph:** idle shows an original **USB-C plug pictogram** in stock's black, rounded style, on every unit (0.8.3: one build, the dock connector is no longer a build setting, #236; owner 2026-11-11 kept today's picture). Until 0.8.2, builds for units with the 30-pin connector (`HAVE_IPOD_DOCK30`) showed stock's 30-pin dock pictogram; the art kit still draws it.
  - The spinning sync arrows during data I/O are the same for both.
  - Both connector glyphs are generated by the art kit.
