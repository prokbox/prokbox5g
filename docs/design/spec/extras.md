# Extras

*Split from `7g-spec.md` "Menu trees" (2026-10-07).*

- **Extras:** **Clocks, Calendars, Contacts, Alarms, Games, Notes, Screen Lock, Stopwatch**, in that (non-alphabetical) order (photos `extras-menu-b.jpg`, `extras-1.jpg`). Split screen with per-item art: **every row has its own icon** (owner, 2026-10-24; ours are original drawings, `apps/ipodui/art/extrasart.py`: Contacts an address book, Alarms a sleek round alarm clock, Games a tabletop of a chess pawn, a six-sided and a four-sided die, Notes a note pad, Screen Lock a padlock, Stopwatch a steel stopwatch; Clocks the map and clock, Calendars the page). **One scale** (owner, second pass 2026-10-24): every drawn icon is about 84 px tall, centred across the panel, its foot on one baseline (panel y 158), with the head-on reflection under it (from 0.375 to nothing over a third of its height, the art's alpha as the mask, computed at draw time; `cv_pal_refl()` in `screens/panels.c`). The Clocks face (96 px) and the Calendars page (80 x 74) set the scale and keep their own drawing. Alarms is itself a **split list** with the alarm clock in the panel on every row (`extras-alarms.jpg`). **Games is split too** (owner, 2026-10-24; a difference: the 7G's Games list is full width with small icons, `extras-games.jpg`): the highlighted game's cover, our own box art with the icons' reflection (`docs/design/sdk.md` §4.4). The covers are 68 x 84 px in the spirit of late-80s / early-90s 8-bit cartridge boxes (owner, 2026-10-24): a chunky 2 x 2 px pixel scene, a coloured frame, a diagonal-stripe band, a checker band at the foot and the title lettered in our own 3 x 5 pixel font; no console maker's marks and no real game's box layout. **The title is baked into the art** (it is the game's name, artwork, not UI text): the Games list prints each game's name as the row's own text beside the cover, so a translation still shows the localised name in the list (rule 8). Alarms holds **Create Alarm** / "New Alarm" and **Sleep Timer** / "Inactive" as two-line rows (owner, 2026-10-22: **Alarms is the entrance for all alarm features**; a new alarm's settings include repeat, the day(s) of the week and snooze); Stopwatch lists New Timer, Clear Logs, then logs (date M/D/YY left, time right in blue).
