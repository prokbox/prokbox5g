# Header bar

*Split from `7g-spec.md` "Global chrome" (2026-10-07). Cross-cutting rules are in [index.md](index.md).*

- **Header bar:** **20 px** (y 0–19), a light silver vertical gradient (#FDFDFD at the top → #B5B5B5 at y 17 → #ABABAB at y 18, with a steep drop around y 8–13), plus a 1 px darker bottom line at y 19 (≈ #6A6A6A).
  - Screen title **left-aligned** at x≈8, bold, black, cap height 9 px (≈ 13 px). *(The earlier spec said centred; the references show left-aligned on light screens. Only the dark full-screen screens centre it: see "Dark full-screen screens".)*
  - Right-aligned glyph cluster, 3 px from the right edge: **play/pause** (blue, 12×12), 5 px gap, **battery** (24×12 body + 2×4 nub, 1 px dark outline; fill colour by charge: **green above 35 %, amber at 35 % or less, red at 20 % or less**, never under a 2 px sliver when red; green while charging; the same bands on the dark header and the big system-screen battery; amber is an owner addition, see `changes-from-7g.md` 3.19; thresholds `UI_BATT_*_PCT` in `header.h`). **On power:** a dark bolt on the fill while charging, a dark plug (12×5, the dark header's shape, light halo) when on power and not charging (charger_inserted()); nothing off power. `MEASUREMENTS.md` (battery state sprites): charging = bolt, plug on a full fill, so the light header now matches the 7G and the dark header. *(The play glyph is on the right next to the battery, not on the left.)*
  - The Hold lock glyph (~9×13) sits left of the play glyph when Hold is on.
  - **Library busy glyph** (ours, `changes-from-7g.md` 3.20): the small 8-dot activity glyph sits at the left end of the right-hand cluster while the library is being updated, whatever started it: the post-USB scan (#84), a boot-time rebuild, a new library format at first boot after an upgrade, Music hold menu › Rebuild Cache or Build Artwork, and the queue's rebase after a commit. A read-only check that finds nothing to change shows nothing. For updates other than post-USB, it comes up only after the work has run **1 s**, then stays at least **1 s**, and phases less than 1 s apart count as one run, so it never blinks. It steps every 125 ms while lit, pushing only its own rect; dark, it neither steps nor draws, and the next lit pass picks up the current state. The library stays browsable throughout: no screen, no splash.
  - On the main menu the header covers only the left (list) half.
  - **Time in Title** (Settings › Date & Time; on in the owner's photos): the header shows the **screen title while the user is moving** (any wheel or button input, and every screen change), then switches to the **clock** ("15:04"; 12/24 h per the 24 Hour Clock setting) about **2–3 s after the last input** (we use 2.5 s), and the clock **stays** until the next input. It never flips back and forth on its own (owner, 2026-10-10, from the owner's 7G; this replaces the earlier timed-alternation reading of `photo 1190.mov`). The clock sits in the title's place (left-aligned on light screens, centred on dark ones). The swap completes within one 24 fps frame (a still caught both ghosted, photo `extras-calendars.jpg`); use a **≤40 ms cross-fade** of the header text only. With Time in Title off, the title stays.
