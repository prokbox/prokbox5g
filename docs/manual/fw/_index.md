This Part is about ProkBox's own firmware: how to get the source, build it, test a change and contribute it. It assumes you are comfortable with C and a command line, but not that you know Rockbox or this player.

**In this edition:**

- [FW-2 From clone to release](02-from-clone-to-release.md): the whole process in one chapter, and a map of the repository.
- [FW-3 Setting up the toolchain](03-toolchain.md): macOS, Linux and containers.
- [FW-4 Building](04-building.md): the simulator, the firmware, the bootloader, and putting a build on a player.
- [FW-8 The CPU and the COP](08-cores.md): what runs on which of the two cores, in 0.8.0 and in the target design.
- [FW-18 Testing](18-testing.md): the gate and the quicker loops.
- [FW-20 Contributing](20-contributing.md): the process, and the legal and naming rules.

Chapters FW-1, FW-5 to FW-7, FW-9 to FW-17 and FW-19 come in later editions. Their numbers are reserved, so the numbers above will not change.
