This is the first public pre-release of ProkBox5g, free firmware for the iPod Video (5th generation and 5.5). It is a pre-release: it works well on the hardware we have tested, but you are among the first to try it. Please read the sections on known hardware and on going back before you install.

> [!NOTE]
> ProkBox5g is not Apple software. It is an independent, community-driven fork of Rockbox, and Apple Inc. has not made, approved, endorsed or supported it in any way. It contains no Apple software. iPod is a trademark of Apple Inc.

## What is in 0.8.0

- **Music.** Browse by artist, album, song, genre and more; Now Playing with cover art, ratings, shuffle and lyrics; On-The-Go playlists; Search; Shuffle Songs. Plays almost any common audio format.
- **Audiobooks.** Chapters, a saved place in every book, and a speed you choose (Slower, Normal, Faster) without changing the pitch of the voice.
- **Drag-and-drop USB.** Plug in, copy files across as with any drive, eject, and the library updates itself. Copy music back off the same way. No sync program.
- **Basic video.** Plays videos you have converted with the included converter, with a clear message when a file can't be played. Subtitles can be burned into the picture by the converter.
- **The visualiser.** A full-screen display that follows the music, in five styles.
- **The interface on the second core** (if bench 4 passes): the menus run on the player's second processor core, so the screen stays smooth while music plays. *(To be confirmed before release.)*
- **Save Diagnostics.** Settings › About › Save Diagnostics writes a small report you can send us if something goes wrong.

## What is not in it yet

These are planned for later releases and are hidden for now:

- Photos
- Album Flow (the album cover browser)
- Sleep and hibernation (the player powers off and starts up the ordinary way)
- Soft subtitles you can switch on and off (use burned-in subtitles instead)
- The games SDK

## Which download do I pick?

There are 8 downloads. Pick the one that matches your player on three points.

| | Choices |
|---|---|
| Battery | **Stock** (the original) or **modded** (a larger replacement) |
| Storage | **Hard drive** (the original) or **flash** (a memory-card or similar adapter) |
| Memory | **32 MB** or **64 MB** |

Each file is named `ProkBox5g-<ver>-{stock,modded}-{flash,hdd}-{32,64}MB.zip`, so the name tells you which one it is for:

| Part of the name | Means | Which to pick |
|---|---|---|
| `stock` or `modded` | Battery | `stock` if it is the original battery, `modded` if you fitted a larger one |
| `flash` or `hdd` | Storage | `hdd` for the original hard drive, `flash` for a memory-card or similar adapter |
| `32MB` or `64MB` | Memory | by the capacity the player was sold with (see below) |

For example, `ProkBox5g-0.8.0-stock-hdd-64MB.zip` is for a player with its original battery and hard drive and 64 MB of memory. Three quick ways to tell which you have:

- **Memory:** the original 30 GB players have 32 MB; the 60 GB and 80 GB players have 64 MB. Flash mods keep the original memory, so go by the capacity the player was sold with.
- **Storage:** if you have never opened the player, it has the original hard drive.
- **Battery:** if you have not replaced it, it is stock.

> [!TIP]
> Not sure? Choose `stock`, `hdd`, and the memory that matches the original capacity, unless you know you changed something.

## Known hardware

0.8.0 has been tested on a **flash-modded 5.5G with 64 MB**. Hard-drive players and 32 MB players should work but have had less testing. **Testers with those are very welcome**, and we especially want to hear from you.

## How to report a problem

1. On the player, choose Settings › About and go to the last page.
2. Choose **Save Diagnostics**. It writes `ProkBox Report.txt` to the top level of the player's drive.
3. Connect the player to your computer and attach that file in an email to **prokbox.project@gmail.com**, or to a new issue on the project's tracker.
4. Tell us what you were doing and what happened.

The report holds the version, hardware details, counts and a short log. It does not hold your music or file names.

## How to go back to Apple's firmware

Apple's own software stays on the player alongside ProkBox.

- **Just for now:** restart (hold **Menu** and **Select** for about 8 seconds), and as soon as the screen goes dark, hold **Menu**.
- **For good, keeping your music:** write the start-up backup you saved at install time back with `ipodpatcher -w ProkBox-backup.bin`.
- **Full restore (erases the player):** put it in Disk Mode and restore it with Apple's own tools.

The full steps are in the guide chapter "Going back to Apple's firmware".

> [!WARNING]
> Back up your music before installing, and before any full restore.
