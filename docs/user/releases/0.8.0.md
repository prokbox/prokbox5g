This is the second public pre-release of ProkBox5g, free firmware for the iPod Video (5th generation and 5.5 generation), and the first with video. It is a pre-release: it works well on the hardware we have tested, but you are among the first to try it. Please read the sections on known hardware and on going back before you install.

> [!NOTE]
> ProkBox5g is not Apple software. It is an independent, community-driven fork of Rockbox, and Apple Inc. has not made, approved, endorsed or supported it in any way. It contains no Apple software. iPod is a trademark of Apple Inc.

## What is in 0.8.0

- **Music.** Browse by artist, album, song, genre and more; Now Playing with cover art, ratings, shuffle and lyrics; On-The-Go playlists; Search; Shuffle Songs. Plays almost any common audio format.
- **Audiobooks.** Chapters, a saved place in every book, and a speed you choose (Slower, Normal, Faster) without changing the pitch of the voice.
- **Drag-and-drop USB.** Plug in, copy files across as with any drive, eject, and the library updates itself. Copy music back off the same way. No sync program.
- **Basic video.** Plays videos you have converted with the included converter, or files already in the player's video format, with a clear message when a file can't be played. Each video keeps its own place, even through a power-off, a flat battery and a USB connection. Posters come from a picture inside the video, a picture with the same name beside it, or `poster.jpg` beside it. Subtitles can be burned into the picture by the converter.
- **Sound and video kept apart.** Play, resume and Now Playing are for sound only: music, audiobooks and audio podcasts. A video starts and resumes only from Videos, and its sound never plays on behind the menus. The screen stays on while a video plays; while it is paused, the usual backlight timeout applies.
- **Audiobook detection.** A file is treated as a book if it is an `.m4b`, has a book genre (English, Dutch or German names), or sits in an `Audiobooks` or `Audio Books` folder.
- **Friendlier defaults.** On fresh settings the backlight is 10 seconds and the click sound is on. Settings you already have are not changed.
- **The visualiser.** A full-screen display that follows the music, in five styles. It never interrupts the music.
- **Save Diagnostics.** Settings › About › Save Diagnostics writes a small report you can send us if something goes wrong.
- **Both processor cores at work.** Music is decoded and sent to the headphones by the player's second processor core, leaving the first free for the menus, the disk and the screen, so the wheel stays quick while music plays.

## What is not in it yet

These are planned for later releases and are hidden for now:

- Photos
- Album Flow (the album cover browser)
- Sleep and hibernation (the player powers off and starts up the ordinary way)
- Soft subtitles you can switch on and off (use burned-in subtitles instead)
- Poster and art folders for whole TV shows and seasons, and one folder per film
- The games SDK

## Which download do I pick?

There are 8 downloads. Pick the one that matches your player on three points.

| | Choices |
|---|---|
| Hardware | **Stock** (original) or **modded** (all three of: 3000 mAh battery, USB-C port, haptic motor) |
| Storage | **Hard drive** (the original) or **flash** (a memory-card or similar adapter) |
| Memory | **32 MB** or **64 MB** |

Each file is named `ProkBox5g-<ver>-{stock,modded}-{flash,hdd}-{32,64}MB.zip`, so the name tells you which one it is for:

| Part of the name | Means | Which to pick |
|---|---|---|
| `stock` or `modded` | Mods | `stock` for an unmodified player, `modded` only if it has the 3000 mAh battery, USB-C and haptics together |
| `flash` or `hdd` | Storage | `hdd` for the original hard drive, `flash` for a memory-card or similar adapter |
| `32MB` or `64MB` | Memory | by the capacity the player was sold with (see below) |

For example, `ProkBox5g-0.8.0-stock-hdd-64MB.zip` is for a player with its original battery and hard drive and 64 MB of memory. Three quick ways to tell which you have:

- **Memory:** the original 30 GB players have 32 MB; the 60 GB and 80 GB players have 64 MB. Flash mods keep the original memory, so go by the capacity the player was sold with.
- **Storage:** if you have never opened the player, it has the original hard drive.
- **Mods:** if you have not opened or changed it, it is stock.

> [!TIP]
> Work out each part before choosing; a wrong storage or memory pick can misbehave or fail to start. Getting started has the checks, and the steps to install, update and go back.

## Known hardware

All eight variants are in this release, but only one has been tested on real hardware: **flash, 64 MB, USB-C and haptics (modded)**. The other seven are **untested**. Hard-drive players, 32 MB players and stock-hardware players should work but have had no testing on a real player. **Testers with those are very welcome**, and we especially want to hear from you.

## Known issues

- **A white flash when you wake the screen of a paused video.**
- **Input lag on the video volume bar and the scrub bar.** The bar can trail the wheel for a moment.
- **Video can stutter for the first moments after a cold start.** It settles by itself; a video started a little later plays smoothly.
- **Several artists in one tag show only the first.** A song tagged with more than one artist is listed under the first.

## Installing

The release carries ready-to-run copies of the install tool (`ProkBox5g-<version>-ipodpatcher-macos-universal`, `-windows-x86_64.exe` and `-linux-x86_64`) next to the eight zips and a `SHA256SUMS` file. There is no installer app yet; Getting started has the steps.

## How to report a problem

1. On the player, choose Settings › About and go to the last page.
2. Choose **Save Diagnostics**. It writes `ProkBox Report.txt` to the top level of the player's drive.
3. Connect the player to your computer and attach that file in an email to **prokbox.project@gmail.com**, or to a new issue on the project's tracker.
4. Tell us what you were doing and what happened.

The report holds the version, hardware details, counts and a short log. It does not hold your music or file names.

## How to go back to Apple's firmware

Apple's own software stays on the player alongside ProkBox. The full steps are in Going back.

- **Just for now:** slide Hold on and restart, or hold **Menu** from the start of the restart.
- **For good, keeping your music:** write back the start-up backup you saved once, before installing, with `ipodpatcher -w` and its file name; or, with no backup, `ipodpatcher -d`.
- **Full restore (erases the player):** put it in Disk Mode and restore it with Apple's own tools.
