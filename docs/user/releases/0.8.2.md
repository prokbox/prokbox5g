ProkBox5g 0.8.2 is a quick bug-fix update to 0.8.1. It stops the player doing pointless background work, makes sure your library is built before anything else, and lets more of your FLAC files play. Your music, settings, places in books and videos, and playlists are kept when you update.

## What is new

- **Your library comes first.** While your library is being built or updated, album covers and other background work now wait until it has finished. The library page shows a short line saying which step it is on, in plain words, and a moving light so it never looks stuck.
- **Full speed for high-quality tracks.** While a track above 48 kHz is playing, the player now holds full speed so the music has the best chance of playing without a break.

## What is fixed

- **Album covers no longer start over and over.** After finishing its artwork pass, the player used to begin another one about every couple of minutes, even though nothing had changed. That used battery and processor time for nothing, and it could get in the way of playback. A pass that changes nothing now ends the work.
- **More FLAC files play.** FLAC files with large blocks (common in high-quality recordings) used to be skipped without any message. Files with blocks of up to 65,535 samples, the largest the format allows, now play.
- **Shuffle on a very small library.** With only one track in the library, Shuffle could get stuck trying the same track over and over, and the screen stopped responding. It now stops cleanly.
- **A queue where nothing can play now stops.** Before, with Repeat on, the player could keep cycling through songs it could not play. It now stops.

## Known issues

- **24-bit/192 kHz and 176.4 kHz FLAC files are likely to cut out every few seconds on this release,** even with the full speed described above. In our tests the player could not keep up with these rates. Lower high-quality rates (88.2 and 96 kHz) and all standard-rate music play normally. A reworked playback engine that fixes this is coming in 0.8.3.
- **A short black screen after sliding Hold off during a video** with Repeat set to All. The video carries on underneath and catches up. Fix planned for 0.8.3.
- **The highest band of some equaliser presets has no effect.** Fix planned for 0.8.3.
- **Japanese, Chinese and Korean characters show as blanks or boxes** in song, artist and album names. Support for them arrives in 0.9.0.
- **A white flash when you wake the screen of a paused video.**
- **Input lag on the video volume bar and the scrub bar.** The bar can trail the wheel for a moment.
- **Several artists in one tag show only the first.** A song tagged with more than one artist is listed under the first.

## Also on the player

- **The Extras menu** has been on the main menu since the first releases: clocks, alarms, a stopwatch, notes, screen lock, contacts and calendars. These notes did not mention it before.

## Updating from 0.8.1

Download the zip for your player (the same one you picked before; the name tells you which), and follow the update steps in Getting started. Your music, settings, places in books and videos, and playlists are kept.

## Known hardware

As with earlier releases, only one variant has been tested on a real player: **flash, 64 MB, USB-C and haptics (modded)**. The other seven are untested, and testers with them are very welcome.
