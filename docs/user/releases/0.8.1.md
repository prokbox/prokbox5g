ProkBox5g 0.8.1 is the first update to the 0.8.0 pre-release. It makes building your music library much faster, shows you what the player is doing while it builds, reads libraries synced by iTunes, and fixes problems found in real use. Your music, settings, places in books and videos, and playlists are kept when you update.

## What is new

- **Much faster library builds.** Building or updating your music library is several times faster on large libraries. In our tests on a simulated player, a 10,000-song library built in about a third of the time it took before, and the gain grows with the size of your collection.
- **A progress page while your library is built.** The first time the player builds your library, and after you unplug it from a computer while it updates, a full-screen page shows a progress bar and what it is doing, in plain words. When it finishes, all of your music is there.
- **Low battery is handled during a build.** Below 15 % battery, a first build starts only on a charger. If you unplug the charger part-way through, the build stops cleanly and carries on later.
- **Faster start-up with large libraries.** The player no longer re-checks your whole library slowly at every start, and the menu appears sooner on big collections.
- **Libraries synced by iTunes.** Songs that iTunes put on the player are listed, with their album covers, alongside the music you copied over yourself. The player only reads them; it never changes what iTunes stored.
- **Bigger libraries.** Up to 50,000 songs on players with 32 MB of memory and 65,000 on players with 64 MB.
- **Shuffle Songs counts your whole library.** Now Playing shows the song's place in all of your music ("12 of 8,431") instead of stopping at 50, and Shuffle still starts straight away.
- **Composers and genres open instantly,** however many albums they hold.
- **Album covers on the main menu.** The Now Playing cover beside the main menu uses the art inside the song file first.
- **Build Artwork keeps the player awake** while it is on a charger, so it finishes.

## What is fixed

- **Leaving a video goes straight back to the list.** Before, the "Preparing Video..." screen could flash up again for a moment when you left a video, whether you pressed MENU or the video reached its end. You now go straight back to the list, and the video still remembers where you were.
- **Hold during a video locks the buttons, nothing else.** Before, sliding Hold on during a video could turn the screen off, stop the picture and, with Repeat on, start the video again. Now the picture and sound carry on and only the buttons are locked. A small padlock appears at the bottom of the screen when you slide Hold on, or press a button while it is on. With Hold on, a video stops at its end instead of repeating.
- **Video starts smoothly from a cold start.** A video started straight after switching on no longer stutters for its first moments.
- **Cover art survives a power cut.** If the player lost power while it was saving cover art, the saved art could be left damaged. The player now finds and repairs such files when it starts.
- **Your library is protected against disk damage.** The player checks the library's files as it writes them and reads them back, so a fault on the disk is caught and repaired instead of quietly damaging your library.
- **Sleep works while the library page is showing.** Holding Play to sleep during a library build now really sleeps the player.
- **A safer end to a video.** If the video processor had already been switched off when a video stopped, the player still tried to read from it. That no longer happens.

## Known issues

- **A short black screen after sliding Hold off during a video** with Repeat set to All. The video carries on underneath and catches up. Fixed in 0.8.2.
- **The highest band of some equaliser presets has no effect.** Fixed in 0.8.2.
- **Japanese, Chinese and Korean characters show as blanks or boxes** in song, artist and album names. Support for them arrives in 0.9.0.
- **A white flash when you wake the screen of a paused video.**
- **Input lag on the video volume bar and the scrub bar.** The bar can trail the wheel for a moment.
- **Several artists in one tag show only the first.** A song tagged with more than one artist is listed under the first.

## Updating from 0.8.0

Download the zip for your player (the same one you picked for 0.8.0; the name tells you which), and follow the update steps in Getting started. Your music, settings, places in books and videos, and playlists are kept. The first start after updating may rebuild your library once, behind the new progress page.

## Known hardware

As with 0.8.0, only one variant has been tested on a real player: **flash, 64 MB, USB-C and haptics (modded)**. The other seven are untested, and testers with them are very welcome.
