# ADR NNNN: Short title of the decision

- Status: Proposed
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD (the date the decision was made)

## Context

What forces led to the question? Two to five sentences.

## Decision

What we do, in plain words.

## Consequences

What gets easier, what gets harder, what must be measured or tested.

## Alternatives considered

- Option: why not.

## Owner decision

The owner's words, quoted from a doc or a tracker issue with its date. Never invent one;
if none is on record, say so and name the commit or document the decision came from.
